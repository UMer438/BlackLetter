# BlackLetter User Manual

## What is BlackLetter?
BlackLetter is an AI-powered legal assistant designed to help you review contracts and legal documents instantly. Instead of reading through pages of dense legal jargon, BlackLetter scans the document for you, identifies risks based on a checklist, and allows you to ask questions in plain English.

Think of it as a "Junior Associate" that never sleeps—it reads the contract, flags the dangerous parts, and answers your questions.

---

## How to Use BlackLetter

### 1. Upload a Document
*   Open the application.
*   You will see a file upload area in the center or left side.
*   Click to select a **PDF contract** from your computer, or drag and drop the file.
*   Wait for the "Processing" indicator to finish.

### 2. Run a Compliance Audit
*   Once the document is uploaded, go to the **Audit** tab (usually the default view).
*   Click the **"Run Compliance Audit"** button.
*   The AI will check the document against a list of common risks (e.g., "Is there unlimited liability?").
*   **Results**:
    *   ✅ **Green Check**: The clause is safe or not found.
    *   ⚠️ **Red Alert**: A risk was found. Click on the alert to read the specific clause and the AI's explanation of *why* it is risky.

### 3. Chat with the Document
*   Have a specific question not in the checklist?
*   Click the **Chat** tab.
*   Type your question in the box, for example:
    *   *"What is the notice period for termination?"*
    *   *"Who pays for return shipping?"*
    *   *"Summarize the payment terms."*
*   The AI will answer based strictly on the content of the uploaded document.

### 4. Customize the Checklist
*   Want to check for something specific?
*   Click the **Checklist** tab.
*   **Add Item**: Type a new question (e.g., *"Is there a non-compete clause?"*) and press Enter or click Add.
*   **Remove Item**: Click the trash icon next to any item you don't need.

### 5. Using on Mobile
*   **Navigation**: The mobile app uses a bottom tab bar to switch between views.
*   **Files Tab**: Upload and view your documents here.
*   **Reader Tab**: View the "Sentences" / Active Document text and analysis highlights.
*   **Audit Tab**: view the risk score and audit checklist results.
